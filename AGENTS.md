## Pre-work gate
Before modifying code, check the canonical skill, repository docs, authoritative base/QA composition, outstanding PRs/dependencies, ERPNext Education behavior, existing EdgeSuite/shared implementation, permission/Branch Eligibility personas and tests, and the smallest bounded mergeable slice.

## EduEdge scope
EduEdge extends ERPNext Education as the system of record. EduEdge owns product-specific education operations, governance, CBT and intelligence where required; it must not duplicate mature ERPNext academic/accounting truth. EdgeSuite is the normal guided surface and authorized native ERPNext remains Advanced. Repository-specific rules extend, but do not silently weaken, the canonical skill.
