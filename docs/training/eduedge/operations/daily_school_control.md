# Daily School Operations and Service Coordination

School Operations coordinates people and services but should not bypass the authorised owner of academic, finance, HR or safety records.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
A[Review branch readiness] --> B[Identify exceptions]
B --> C[Assign responsible owner]
C --> D[Communicate approved action]
D --> E[Record outcome or escalate]
```

## Daily control

1. Review timetable, staffing, rooms, transport, utilities, events and open incidents.
2. Confirm the correct branch and accountable service owner.
3. Assign clear action, owner and deadline.
4. Send notices only to verified recipients using approved wording.
5. Close the issue with evidence or escalate it before the deadline.

## Boundaries

- Operations coordinates; teachers own teaching records.
- Bursary owns accounting entries and receipts.
- HR owns confidential employment records.
- Safety officers control student release and incident evidence.

## Practice Exercise

Prepare a morning readiness review for a campus with a teacher absence, unavailable classroom, delayed school bus and planned examination.
