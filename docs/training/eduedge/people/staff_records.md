# HR Staff Records and Onboarding

This module is available only when Frappe HRMS and the Employee DocType are installed.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
A[Verify identity and employment] --> B[Create Employee]
B --> C[Assign department and branch]
C --> D[Complete onboarding and policies]
D --> E[Coordinate system roles separately]
```

## Operating procedure

1. Verify identity, contact, department, designation, company and employment dates.
2. Create one Employee record and avoid duplicate identities.
3. Record the correct branch or work location and reporting line.
4. Complete document, safeguarding, policy and role-specific onboarding.
5. Ask authorised administrators to grant system roles and campus access separately.

## Confidentiality

- HR employment data does not automatically grant application access.
- Restrict personal, disciplinary, medical and payroll information.
- Keep onboarding evidence and acknowledgement dates.

## Practice Exercise

Prepare an onboarding checklist for a new teacher who will work at two campuses but teach only one programme at each campus.
